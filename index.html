Two operational systems designed, built and rolled out for real teams. Simple Stock tracks every item from supplier order to stock-room shelf, and Cove Laundry takes a guest's basket from the room to the folding table and back with every piece accounted for. Rounding it out is The Last Conjuration, a short horror game and a reminder that games are where all of this started.

Simple Stock

One stock system for the office and the stock room. Compare supplier prices, order, book deliveries in against the order, book stock out to named workers, run stock takes and print a PDF of all of it.

Cove Laundry

Guests scan a code in their room, photograph their basket and list every item, with nothing to install. Staff collect it, tick each piece off, wash, dry and fold, and the guest is notified the moment it's done.

Platform
Android app for staff, web page for guests
Built with
React Native, Expo, Supabase
