Just Kuz Projects. Apps for the people doing the actual work.

I'm an app developer based in South Africa. For the past year I've been designing and building the apps that run day-to-day operations for a facilities-management client. Such as job tracking, cleaning, laundry, stock and procurement, used every day by a team of 30 field workers.

I didn't take the straight route into tech. After finishing school in 2019 it took me a while to work out what I wanted to do. Once I started building things, first games and small personal apps, then tools for businesses.

Most of what I've learned has come from shipping software people depend on.

How I work

I start with the people using it

Before writing any code I find out how the job is done today: who does it, on what device, and where it goes wrong.

You see something working early

You get a rough working version within the first couple of weeks, not a long spec document, so we can change direction cheaply.

Security from day one

Every app gets access rules on its data from the start, so staff only see what they should and customers can't see each other's details.

Built for real conditions

Patchy signal, shared tablets, gloves, cracked screens. Big buttons, offline saving and nothing that needs a manual.
