How a project runs

  1. Free chat

    You tell me how things work today and what's getting in the way. About 30 minutes, by call or in person.

  2. Plan and quote

    I send a short plan of what the app will do, with screens sketched out and a fixed price.

  3. Build and test with your team

    You get a working version early. Your staff try it on their own phones and we adjust as we go.

  4. Launch

    I install it on your devices, set up your data and show everyone how to use it.

  5. Support

    I fix anything that comes up and help the app grow with your business.

Common questions

Do I need to buy servers or software?

No. Your app's data is stored with a cloud database provider. For most small businesses the free or entry level plan is enough, and I'll tell you up front if you'll need to pay for anything.

Will it work on iPhones?

Yes, the apps can be built for both Android and iPhone. Putting an app on iPhones needs an Apple developer account, which Apple charges a yearly fee for. Many businesses start with Android only, or use a web version for iPhone users.

Who owns the app and the data?

You do. The data lives in an account in your business's name, and you can export it at any time.

How long does it take?

A Personal app usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, and a Starter app 2 to 3 weeks. A Business app takes 4 to 8 weeks, depending on how much it needs to do.

How far ahead should I order a custom game?

Games take 3 to 5 weeks, depending on availability, so get in touch at least 5 weeks before the date, especially for a proposal or a birthday. That leaves time for you to play it first and ask for changes.

What if my staff aren't good with phones?

That's who I design for. The apps I've built are used daily by field workers with large buttons, plain words and as few steps as possible.
